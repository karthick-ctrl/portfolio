Face Recognition Pipeline — End-to-End Flow

PIPELINE STAGES & MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION

1. OpenCV Haar / DNN Detection: Locates bounding box coordinates in raw video frames with spatial downscaling.
2. Dlib 68 Facial Landmarks: Standardizes eye, nose, and jaw fiducial coordinates for affine transformation and rotational alignment.
3. HOG 128D Vector Extraction: Generates Histogram of Oriented Gradients capturing structural facial contour geometry invariant to skin tone and lighting.
4. SVM Decision Hyperplane: Multi-class Radial Basis Function (RBF) Support Vector Machine computes classification probabilities.

DocuMind AI — RAG Architecture & Vector Pipeline

RETRIEVAL-AUGMENTED GENERATION (RAG) STAGES

1. Ingestion & Semantic Chunking: Extracts raw text from multi-page PDFs using PyPDF and applies RecursiveCharacterTextSplitter (chunk size: 800, overlap: 150) preserving section context.
2. Dense Vector Embeddings: Computes high-dimensional dense embeddings and indexes into FAISS (Facebook AI Similarity Search) with IndexFlatIP / Cosine metric.
3. Hybrid Similarity Search & Re-ranking: Top-K retrieved chunks are filtered, re-ranked based on query intent similarity, and matched to specific page coordinates.
4. Context-Injected Generation: Prompts the LLM with strict grounding instructions: answer strictly based on retrieved context and append verifiable [Source X, Page Y] citation anchors.
